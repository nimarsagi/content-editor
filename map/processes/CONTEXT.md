# processes/ — the verbs

Five movements that actually run. Nothing here is aspirational.

| Verb | What it is | Card |
|---|---|---|
| **run** | one video in, one captioned video out | [run](run.md) |
| **redo** | rebuild from a transcript you corrected — without re-transcribing | [redo](redo.md) |
| **calibrate** | level the audio; also the tune-by-ear loop, run on its own | [calibrate](calibrate.md) |
| **learn** | turn a corrected word into a lexicon proposal | [learn](learn.md) |
| **check** | syntax plus the end-to-end fixture | [check](check.md) |

Only `run` and `redo` are commands the person types often. `calibrate` runs inside `run` and standalone; `learn` is what you do after noticing something; `check` mostly fires by itself.

There is no sixth. The cut-proposal and unsupervised-mode loops are gone — `BUILD-NOTES.md`, *What changed on 2026-07-29*.
